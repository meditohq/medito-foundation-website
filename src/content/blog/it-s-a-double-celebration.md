#### In celebration, we're giving away a bundle of our favourite books!

**Alan Watts - The Book on the Taboo Against Knowing Who You Are**
In this book, Watts explores an unrecognised but mighty taboo - our tacit conspiracy to ignore who, or what, we really are. Alan Watts, key thinker of Western Zen Buddhism, explains how to reconsider our relationship with the world.

**Eckhart Tolle - The Power of Now**
A renowned councillor and spiritual teacher, Eckhart Tolle experienced a profound spiritual revelation at the age of twenty-nine that completely changed his outlook on life. Sharing his message in The Power of Now, he has inspired a generation to live more fully in the present in a more mindful, engaged and fulfilling way.

**Pema Chödrön - Start Where You Are**
In this perennial self-help bestseller, Pema Chödrön works with traditional Tibetan Buddhist slogans as everyday meditations. Start Where You Are shows how we can all develop the courage to work with our own inner pain and discover true joy, holistic well-being, and unshakeable confidence.

**Yongey Mingyur Rinpoche & Eric Swanson - The Joy of Living**
This groundbreaking book invites us to join in unlocking the secrets behind the practice of meditation. Working with neuroscientists, the author provides clear insights into modern research, which indicates that systematic training in meditation can enhance activity in areas of the brain associated with happiness and compassion.
