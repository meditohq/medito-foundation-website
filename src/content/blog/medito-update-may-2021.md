### Medito: The only 100% free, nonprofit, open source meditation app in the world
![Free, nonprofit, open source](https://meditofoundation.org/media/pages/blog/medito-update-may-2021/01e0b35d7c-1622973831/world.gif)

We've been doing some research, and we can't find any other app that is 100% free, nonprofit and open source. We believe that it's important for meditation apps to have all of these characteristics. Take a look at our our [comparison of popular meditation apps](https://meditofoundation.org/blog/meditation-apps-free-nonprofit-open-source) and read our explanation of why meditation apps should be 100% free, nonprofit and open source.

If you find any other app that meets all three criteria, then [please let us know](/contact#general-enquiries)!
