It is with mixed emotions that we announce the departure of two of our esteemed board members, Gareth and Steven Yorke, from Medito Foundation. Both Gareth and Steven have been integral to our journey since the very inception of our project, even before we transformed Medito into a nonprofit foundation.

Gareth and Steven played pivotal roles in the creation of our meditation and mindfulness content, working closely with our Scientific and Diversity & Inclusion Boards. Gareth, with his exceptional skills, managed various projects and finances, ensuring smooth operations and fiscal responsibility. Steven, on the other hand, helped us forge partnerships with leading researchers in the mindfulness and meditation world, including collaborations with universities like Bath University. He also significantly contributed to our social media efforts, particularly our YouTube channel, helping to spread our message and grow our community.

Their contributions have been invaluable, and their impact on Medito is indelible. The foundation, our app, and the community have all been profoundly shaped by their dedication and hard work. Gareth and Steven’s vision and efforts have left an enduring mark on Medito, and they will always be a cherished part of our history.

We part ways with the utmost respect and gratitude for their service. We sincerely wish Gareth and Steven all the best in their future endeavours and look forward to seeing the positive impact they will undoubtedly continue to make.

As we move forward, we remain committed to our mission of providing free, accessible meditation and mindfulness resources to all. We are confident that, with the continued support of our community, we will honor Gareth and Steven’s legacy and build upon the strong foundation they helped create.

Thank you, Gareth and Steven, for your immense contributions. You will always be a part of Medito.

With gratitude,
The Team and Board of Medito Foundation
