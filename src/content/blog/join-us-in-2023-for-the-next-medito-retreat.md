Meet like-minded people in a beautiful woodland setting, practice mindfulness and relax! This is hosted on a nonprofit basis, so any surplus proceeds will go to Medito Foundation, to help us to deliver free mindfulness resources to the world. The Medito team leading the retreat are volunteers.

**The 2023 retreats have now sold out. If you are interested in joining us for our next retreats in 2024, [enter your email address here](https://form.typeform.com/to/kG3l5VIJ) to be the first to hear when we release the details.**

### Location
Barefoot & Bower, Ryelands, Gloucester, UK

### Number of spaces available
Maximum 15 people per retreat

### Dates
- The first retreat took place **26th - 29th May 2023**. Want to see what it was like? [Watch the video](https://youtu.be/8pmNCGNz0ZQ)

- The second retreat took place on **8th - 11th September 2023**. [Watch video now](https://youtu.be/wNVoGLKrui8?si=lWt-pEIweXX6ZCfn).

### Food
We will provide healthy, plant-based meals: breakfast, lunch and dinner, tea and coffee, snacks. Please advise us of any dietary requirements.

### Activities
Meditation, mindful walking, swimming in the lake and talks on mindfulness, compassion and insight from the founders of Medito Foundation.

### Availability

This is a non-profit event, so any surplus proceeds will go back into Medito Foundation.
