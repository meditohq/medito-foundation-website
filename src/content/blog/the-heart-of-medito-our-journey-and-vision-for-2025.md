In a world increasingly divided by extreme viewpoints and political tensions, the need for mental clarity and emotional balance has never been more crucial. For nearly six years, Medito has stood as more than just a meditation app – we've been a beacon of accessibility in mental wellness, believing that everyone deserves access to tools that can improve their mental wellbeing, regardless of their financial situation or geographical location.

### A Milestone Worth Celebrating

As we step into 2025, we're thrilled to share some good news: we've successfully closed our 2024 funding gap. This achievement means more than just numbers on a spreadsheet – it represents our community's collective commitment to making mindfulness accessible during these challenging times, when political polarisation and social tensions are affecting mental health worldwide.

### The Reality of Being a Nonprofit in a Divided World

Many assume that because we're a nonprofit, we don't need money to operate. The reality is more nuanced. While for-profit meditation apps focus on maximising shareholder value, we channel every dollar back into our mission. Yet we still face the same challenges: server costs, development expenses, and the need to support our dedicated team members who, like everyone else, have bills to pay.

Our annual operating costs of $120,000 might seem modest compared to the multi-million dollar budgets of commercial meditation apps. But this modest sum enables us to compete in a landscape dominated by well-funded alternatives, all while staying true to our core principle: meditation should be free and accessible to everyone, everywhere – from regions experiencing political upheaval to communities grappling with social change.

### Growing Pains and Honest Reflections

The past year has been transformative, not just for us but for the world at large. As global tensions rise and extreme views dominate headlines, we've recognized our responsibility to provide a digital sanctuary for mental wellness. What began as a passion project has evolved into something far more significant – a tool for promoting understanding and balance in an increasingly polarized world.

By mid-2023, we had grown to accommodate up to 60 collaborators – a scale that brought both opportunities and obstacles. This rapid growth taught us valuable lessons about maintaining our core mission while adapting to a world that desperately needs tools for mental clarity and emotional regulation.

### Your Voice, Our Direction

For 2025, we're returning to our roots while expanding our impact in these turbulent times. Our community's feedback, combined with our observation of global needs, has shaped our immediate priorities:

1. We're dedicating significant resources to fixing recurring bugs, ensuring your meditation practice remains uninterrupted when you need it most.
2. We're recommitting to simplicity – in our app design, our operations, and our mission – because in a complex world, we believe clarity begins with simplicity.
3. We're exploring additional wellness services and products that could provide sustainable revenue while keeping our core meditation content completely free. These optional offerings will complement your meditation journey without ever compromising our fundamental promise: quality meditation guidance available to everyone, regardless of their financial situation or global circumstances.

### A Message of Gratitude

First and foremost, we want to express our deepest gratitude to everyone who helped us close our 2024 funding gap. Your generosity ensures that Medito can continue to be there for people during their most challenging moments – whether they're dealing with workplace stress, relationship difficulties, health concerns, or simply seeking a moment of peace in their daily lives.

### The Science Behind Our Mission

Our impact isn't just anecdotal – [it's scientifically proven](https://www.bath.ac.uk/announcements/just-10-minutes-of-mindfulness-daily-boosts-wellbeing-and-fights-depression/). A recent study by the Universities of Bath and Southampton showed that just 10 minutes of daily mindfulness practice with Medito led to significant improvements in mental wellbeing. Participants experienced reduced depression, decreased anxiety, and improved overall wellbeing – benefits that lasted well beyond the study period.

As we move forward, we carry not just the lessons of our past, but also a deep understanding of what our community needs. Your support – whether through donations, feedback, or simply using and sharing Medito – helps create moments of peace and clarity for people from all walks of life.

### Be Part of Something Meaningful

Every time you open Medito to meditate, you're already supporting our mission by being part of our growing community of mindful individuals. Your practice joins thousands of others who are taking time each day to care for their mental wellbeing – parents finding moments of calm between responsibilities, students managing exam stress, professionals balancing demanding careers, and anyone seeking tools to navigate life's ups and downs.

Think of it this way: your morning coffee might cost $5, but that same amount could help provide free meditation guidance to dozens of people who might not otherwise have access to these transformative tools. Every contribution, no matter the size, helps write the next chapter of our story and brings moments of peace to those who need them most.
