Thanks to the thousands of insights from our recent research survey, we’ve listened closely to your thoughts, suggestions, and ideas. We’re excited to share our fresh approach with you. And don’t worry—**Medito will always remain ad-free and content free, because we believe mental wellbeing is a universal right, not a commodity.**

### Keeping What Matters Most Intact
Your experience on Medito is sacred. **That’s why we’re keeping our core offerings exactly as they are: completely free, uninterrupted meditation sessions that you can enjoy anytime, anywhere.** Our mission is to help you cultivate mindfulness without any distractions, and we’re working hard to continue adding even more free content—though as you know, quality content takes time.

### A Thoughtful Twist on Premium Features
While the foundation of Medito remains unchanged, we’re exploring a new way to support our work through non-essential subscription features. Think of it as a little extra spark—features that enhance your daily practice without ever interfering with your core experience. Imagine a handy streak freeze that keeps your progress intact when life gets hectic, or home screen widgets that remind you to take that mindful moment even on your busiest days. And don’t worry—these enhancements are coming soon, with rollouts scheduled over the next few weeks and months.

### Extra Benefits for Extra Support
These optional subscription features are designed solely to provide added benefits for those who wish to contribute further to the project. They’re a way for you to support Medito’s mission while enjoying some bonus perks, **without ever putting our free meditation content behind a paywall**. For our valued donors, rest assured—you’ll be among the first to access these upcoming extras.[We’re also still welcoming donations](https://donate.meditofoundation.org) because every bit of support helps us keep Medito thriving and accessible to all.

### Moving Forward, Together
This thoughtful evolution is all about balance: maintaining our core commitment to free, ad-free meditation, while gently exploring avenues that allow us to invest in new, innovative features. Your voice has been our guide, and we are excited to build a future that respects our values and meets your needs.

Thank you for being a part of our journey. Our commitment to making mindfulness and meditation accessible to everyone remains at the heart of everything we do. Together, we’re building a more mindful and compassionate world—one person at a time.

Stay mindful
