#### 3. A shiny new app interface is rolling out 💪
![Shiny new app](https://meditofoundation.org/media/pages/blog/medito-april-update-2021-birthday-special/ea531152c1-1619347932/phone-gif.gif)
We've started rolling out the new app design! It will be released in phases to make sure it is all working correctly and you have the best experience possible. If you haven't already got the update, it will be available within the next two weeks. The new version of the app includes a home screen with quick links to the most popular sections and an easier way to navigate through the main courses. There is also a new 'Library' section where you can easily find your downloaded sessions.
