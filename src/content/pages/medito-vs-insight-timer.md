## Two free options, different trade-offs

**Insight Timer** is one of the largest meditation apps, with a massive library of community-uploaded content. It offers a generous free tier with ads and a premium subscription at $59.99/year for an ad-free experience and courses.

**Medito** is a free, open-source meditation app by a non-profit foundation. All content is curated, free, and there are no ads at any tier.

## Feature comparison

| Feature | Medito | Insight Timer |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| Price | Free, forever | Free tier + $59.99/year premium |
| Ads | None | Yes (free tier) |
| Account required | No | Yes |
| Content library | Curated, hundreds | Community-uploaded, 200K+ |
| Sleep content | Yes, free | Mixed free/premium |
| Courses | Yes, free | Premium only |
| Meditation timer | Yes, free | Yes, free |
| Social features | No | Yes (groups, friends) |
| Content quality | Curated, reviewed | Varies (community content) |
| Open source | Yes | No |
| Non-profit | Yes | No |

## Who is Insight Timer for?

Insight Timer is great if you want a massive variety of content from thousands of different teachers. The community features (groups, friends, milestones) add a social element to meditation. However, the free tier includes ads, courses require premium, and content quality varies since anyone can upload.

## Who is Medito for?

Medito is for people who want a focused, ad-free experience with curated content. Every meditation is reviewed for quality. There's no social layer or community uploads — just a clean, distraction-free meditation app. Everything is free, no exceptions.

## The bottom line

If you want variety and community, Insight Timer has the larger library. If you want a curated, ad-free, no-account experience where everything is genuinely free, Medito is the simpler choice.
