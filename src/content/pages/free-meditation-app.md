## Most "free" meditation apps aren't really free

You've probably tried a few. You download the app, start a meditation, and then hit the paywall. "Upgrade to premium for $12.99/month." The free tier gives you a handful of sessions and locks everything else.

Medito is different. Every single meditation, course, sleep story, and feature is free. Not free for 7 days. Not free with limitations. Free. Forever.

## Why Medito is free

Medito Foundation is a registered non-profit. We don't have shareholders, growth targets, or a premium tier to upsell you toward.

We believe that learning to take care of your mind shouldn't depend on your ability to pay. That's not a marketing line — it's our legal charter as a foundation.

## What you get for free

Everything. There is no premium version. Every user gets:

- **Guided meditations** across dozens of categories
- **Beginner courses** to learn from scratch
- **30-day challenges** to build a lasting habit
- **Sleep stories and sounds** to help you drift off
- **Breathing exercises** for quick stress relief
- **Meditation timer** with ambient sounds for unguided practice
- **Daily meditations** updated regularly

## No account required

You don't need to sign up, enter your email, or create a profile. Download the app and start meditating in under 30 seconds.

## No ads, no tracking dark patterns

We don't show ads. We don't sell your data. We don't use manipulative design to keep you hooked. Our app is also fully open source — you can inspect every line of code yourself.
