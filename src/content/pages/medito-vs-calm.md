## Two apps, two very different models

**Calm** is one of the most popular meditation apps in the world, with a polished experience and celebrity-narrated content. It operates as a for-profit company with a subscription model starting at $69.99/year.

**Medito** is a free, open-source meditation app built by a non-profit foundation. Every feature and piece of content is free, with no subscription, no ads, and no account required.

Both apps aim to help people meditate. The difference is in the model.

## Who is Calm for?

Calm is a great app if you value celebrity narrations (like LeBron James or Matthew McConaughey), highly produced content, and don't mind paying for a subscription. It has a large content library and a well-designed experience.

## Who is Medito for?

Medito is for anyone who wants to meditate without financial barriers. If you want quality guided meditations, sleep content, and courses without ever being asked to pay, Medito is the alternative. It's built by a registered non-profit, funded by optional donations from the community.

## The bottom line

If budget is a factor, Medito gives you everything you need to build a meditation practice without spending a penny. If you specifically want Calm's premium content and celebrity narrators, that's what the subscription is for.
