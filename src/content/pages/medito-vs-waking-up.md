## Philosophy meets accessibility

**Waking Up** is a meditation app created by Sam Harris, a neuroscientist, philosopher, and author. It goes beyond basic meditation, exploring the nature of consciousness, the self, and philosophical inquiry. It costs $99.99/year, though a free scholarship is available for those who can't afford it.

**Medito** is a free, open-source meditation app built by a non-profit foundation. All content is free, with no subscription, no account required, and no need to apply for access.

## Who is Waking Up for?

Waking Up is ideal if you're interested in the intellectual and philosophical side of meditation — consciousness, the illusion of self, and conversations with leading thinkers. Sam Harris brings a unique secular, scientific perspective. The scholarship programme makes it accessible to those who apply, but the default is a paid subscription.

## Who is Medito for?

Medito is for anyone who wants practical meditation tools without barriers. There's no philosophical curriculum, but there are hundreds of guided sessions, courses, sleep content, and breathing exercises — all free by default, for everyone, no application needed.

## The bottom line

Waking Up offers a deep intellectual exploration of meditation and consciousness for those willing to pay or apply for a scholarship. Medito offers a comprehensive, practical meditation experience that's free for everyone out of the box.
