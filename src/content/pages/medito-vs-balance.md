## Personalised vs accessible

**Balance** is a meditation app that personalises your experience based on your goals and preferences. It uses daily check-ins to tailor sessions to how you're feeling. It offers a free first year, then $69.99/year.

**Medito** is a free, open-source meditation app built by a non-profit. All content is free forever, with no account required.

## Feature comparison

| Feature | Medito | Balance |
|---------|--------|---------|
| Price | Free, forever | Free 1st year, then $69.99/year |
| Account required | No | Yes |
| Personalisation | Browse by category | AI-personalised daily plans |
| Guided meditations | Hundreds | Personalised sessions |

| Ads | None | None |

## Who is Balance for?

Balance is a good choice if you want a highly personalised experience that adapts to your mood and goals each day. The first year is free, which is generous, but the subscription kicks in after that.

## Who is Medito for?

Medito is for anyone who wants a straightforward meditation app that will always be free. There's no personalisation engine, but there are hundreds of sessions across categories like stress, sleep, anxiety, and beginner courses. No account, no eventual paywall.

## The bottom line

Balance offers a tailored experience but at a cost after year one. Medito offers a comprehensive meditation library that's free today, tomorrow, and always.
