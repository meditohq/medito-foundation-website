## Skeptics welcome, either way

**Ten Percent Happier** was founded by Dan Harris, an ABC News anchor who had a panic attack on live TV and discovered meditation. The app takes a practical, no-nonsense approach aimed at skeptics. It costs $99.99/year.

**Medito** is a free, open-source meditation app built by a non-profit foundation. All features are free, with no subscription or account required.

## Who is Ten Percent Happier for?

Ten Percent Happier is ideal if you want a sceptic-friendly approach, live coaching, and access to well-known meditation teachers like Joseph Goldstein and Sharon Salzberg. It's a premium product with a premium price.

## Who is Medito for?

Medito is for anyone who wants to meditate without barriers. No celebrities, no live coaching, but hundreds of high-quality guided meditations, courses, and sleep content — all free. Built by people who believe mindfulness shouldn't have a price tag.

## The bottom line

Ten Percent Happier offers a unique editorial voice and live features for those willing to pay. Medito offers a complete meditation experience for free, backed by a non-profit mission.
