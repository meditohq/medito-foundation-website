## Two approaches to mindfulness

**Headspace** is a well-known meditation app co-founded by Andy Puddicombe, a former Buddhist monk. It uses animations and a friendly tone to make meditation approachable. It operates as a for-profit company with a subscription starting at $69.99/year.

**Medito** is a free, open-source meditation app built by a non-profit foundation. All content is free, no account is required, and there are no ads or upsells.

## Who is Headspace for?

Headspace is ideal if you enjoy animated guides, a structured curriculum with Andy Puddicombe's teaching style, and features like focus music and move workouts. It's a comprehensive wellness platform, but most features require a subscription.

## Who is Medito for?

Medito is for anyone who wants straightforward, high-quality meditation content without paying. There are no animations or gamification — just guided meditations, courses, sleep content, and breathing exercises. All free, all the time.

## The bottom line

If you want a polished, animated wellness platform and don't mind the subscription, Headspace delivers. If you want the core meditation experience without any cost, Medito has you covered.
